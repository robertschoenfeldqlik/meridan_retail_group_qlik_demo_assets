## 1. Policy Statement

Meridian Commerce Group is committed to full compliance with EU food safety regulations across all markets where we sell food products. We will maintain the ability to trace any food product one step back (to our supplier) and one step forward (to our customer or retail location) as required by Article 18 of Regulation (EC) No 178/2002.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all MCG food and beverage products, including:
- Fresh produce (fruits, vegetables, herbs)
- Dairy products
- Meat and fish (chilled and frozen)
- Packaged grocery items
- Health and wellness products containing food-grade ingredients

## 3. Traceability Requirements

### 3.1 Mandatory Data Capture

For every food product lot received at an MCG facility, the following data MUST be captured and retained:

| Data Element | Requirement | Retention Period | Current Compliance Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supplier identity | Name, address, registration number | 5 years | 100% |
| Product description | SKU, product name, category | 5 years | 100% |
| Lot/batch number | Supplier-assigned lot ID | 5 years | **85%** (target: 100%) |
| Quantity received | Units, weight, volume as appropriate | 5 years | 98% |
| Date of receipt | Date and time of DC receipt | 5 years | 97% |
| Country/farm of origin | Country code + facility ID where possible | 5 years | **88%** (target: 100%) |
| Delivery destination | Store ID or customer order ID | 5 years | **70%** (target: 100%) |
| Shelf/display date | Date product placed for sale | 5 years | **32%** (target: 100%) |
| Expiry/best-before date | As labeled by supplier | 5 years | 95% |

### 3.2 Sub-Tier Supplier Visibility

EU regulations require traceability to the immediate supplier. MCG's voluntary commitment extends to mapping sub-tier suppliers (farms, processors) for high-risk categories:

| Category | Direct Suppliers | Known Sub-Tier Suppliers | Sub-Tier Visibility |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fresh produce | 120 | 180 of ~450 estimated | **40%** |
| Dairy | 85 | 95 of ~200 estimated | **48%** |
| Meat & fish | 62 | 110 of ~280 estimated | **39%** |
| Packaged grocery | 73 | 45 of ~270 estimated | **17%** |
| **Total food** | **340** | **430 of ~1,200 estimated** | **36%** |

**Target**: 80% sub-tier visibility by December 2026. Current initiative: Supplier Transparency Program (jointly managed by Procurement and Compliance).

### 3.3 Recall Readiness

Per SOP-SC-001 (Product Recall Procedure), MCG must be able to identify all affected retail locations within 24 hours of a recall trigger.

**Current capability assessment (post-RCL-2025-003 lettuce recall):**
- Average time to trace a food product from DC to all receiving stores: **6-11 days**
- Target: **< 24 hours**
- Gap: Traceability data is fragmented across 4 systems (SAP, Oracle WMS, supplier portal, quality management system) with no unified query capability
- Remediation: Project Qlarity (Qlik platform implementation) will provide unified traceability lineage. Target: Q2 2026.

## 4. Compliance Monitoring

### 4.1 Internal Audits

- Monthly spot audits: Random selection of 50 food SKU receipts per DC, verified for complete traceability data
- Quarterly compliance report to the Board Audit Committee
- Annual comprehensive audit by Internal Audit team

### 4.2 Regulatory Audits

- EU Member State food safety authorities may conduct unannounced inspections
- MCG must provide traceability data within 4 hours of regulatory request
- **Audit history:**

| Date | Authority | Scope | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2024-03-15 | NVWA (Netherlands) | Routine inspection, Rotterdam DC | PASS with 2 minor observations |
| 2025-02-20 | NVWA (Netherlands) | Follow-up on consumer complaint | PASS |
| 2025-06-10 | NVWA (Netherlands) | Triggered by RCL-2025-003 recall | **FAIL -- Citation issued** |
| 2025-09-15 | BVL (Germany) | Routine inspection, Munich store cluster | PASS with 1 observation |

### 4.3 Corrective Action Plan (Post-Citation)

Following the June 2025 NVWA citation, MCG submitted a corrective action plan with these commitments:

1. **Immediate (completed)**: Mandatory lot number capture at all DCs (SOP-WH-001 v3.0+)
2. **60 days (completed)**: Store-level receipt tracking improvement to > 85%
3. **90 days (in progress)**: Unified traceability system implementation plan (Project Qlarity)
4. **12 months (in progress)**: Full sub-tier supplier mapping for fresh produce

The next NVWA follow-up inspection is expected in Q1 2026.

## 5. Non-Compliance Consequences

| Severity | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Missing lot number at receipt | Product held until lot number obtained from supplier |
| Missing country of origin for food product | Compliance incident report filed; supplier placed on watch list |
| Failure to provide traceability data within 4 hours of regulatory request | Escalation to Chief Compliance Officer; potential regulatory fine |
| Repeat non-compliance | Supplier contract review; disciplinary action for responsible staff |
