## 1. Purpose

This communication serves as formal notice that Meridian Commerce Group requires TransFrio Transportes Ltda. to implement mandatory pre-cooling to **0°C** for all refrigerated shipments on the Sao Paulo-Buenos Aires corridor, effective **immediately**.

This requirement supersedes the previous pre-cool standard of 2°C specified in the Master Service Agreement (MSA-2024-TF-001, Section 7.2).

## 2. Background

Since August 2025, MCG has experienced a **340% increase in temperature exceedances** on shipments transported by TransFrio on the GRU-BA route. Our investigation (INC-2025-05102) identified that the root cause is a **15-minute refrigeration startup gap** in TransFrio's newly upgraded Thermo King T-series fleet, compounded by increased border crossing wait times at Uruguaiana.

The financial impact to MCG in Q3 2025 was **$2.48M in spoilage losses**, with ongoing exposure estimated at **$180K per week** if uncorrected.

## 3. Mandatory Requirements

### 3.1 Immediate (Effective October 15, 2025)

| Requirement | Detail | Verification |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-cool temperature | 0°C (measured at center of trailer interior) | MCG Tive sensor reading at load-out |
| Pre-cool verification | TransFrio driver must present reefer unit display showing 0°C to MCG loading supervisor before any product is loaded | Supervisor sign-off on Bill of Lading |
| Engine-off protocol | Refrigeration unit must remain ON at all times during transit, including during border crossing waits | Tive sensor continuous monitoring |
| Exceedance notification | TransFrio must notify MCG within 30 minutes of any reefer unit alarm or failure | Email to latam-coldchain@mcg-logistics.com |

### 3.2 Within 30 Days (By November 15, 2025)

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Auxiliary Power Unit (APU) | TransFrio to install APU on all trucks serving the GRU-BA route to maintain refrigeration during engine-off periods |
| Driver training | All TransFrio drivers on GRU-BA route to complete MCG cold chain awareness training (TRN-2025-002) |
| Firmware update | TransFrio to work with Thermo King on firmware update to reduce startup gap from 15 to 5 minutes (per TransFrio's own timeline) |

### 3.3 Within 60 Days (By December 15, 2025)

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Real-time telemetry sharing | TransFrio to provide API access to their fleet reefer unit telemetry data, enabling MCG to correlate with Tive sensor data |
| Route contingency plan | TransFrio to provide documented contingency plan for border crossing delays > 2 hours, including pre-positioned fuel for reefer units |

## 4. Financial Terms

### 4.1 Pre-Cool Surcharge

MCG acknowledges TransFrio's estimate of 15% increased energy costs for 0°C pre-cooling. MCG is prepared to negotiate a surcharge of up to **BRL 120 per shipment** (not the BRL 180 proposed by TransFrio) contingent on TransFrio meeting all requirements in Section 3.

### 4.2 Penalty Structure

Effective November 1, 2025:

| Breach | Penalty |
|---|---|
| Shipment arrives with product above temperature limit | 100% of shipment freight cost credited to MCG |
| Pre-cool verification failed (truck not at 0°C at load-out) | Shipment refused. TransFrio bears all costs for return and rescheduling. |
| Reefer unit failure during transit (> 30 min downtime) | $500 per incident + product replacement cost |
| Failure to notify MCG of reefer alarm within 30 minutes | $250 per incident |

### 4.3 Contract Continuation

Per MSA-2024-TF-001, Section 12.3 (Performance Review), MCG reserves the right to reduce volume allocation or terminate the agreement with 60 days' notice if:
- Temperature exceedances on GRU-BA route do not fall below 3 per week by December 31, 2025
- Product rejection rate does not return below 5% by December 31, 2025

MCG is actively evaluating air freight alternatives for high-value perishables on this route as a contingency.
