## 3. Key Issues

### 3.1 Quality Rejection Rate Spike

Quality rejection rate more than doubled from 2.1% to 4.8% in Q3. Analysis by product:

| Product | Rejection Rate | Primary Cause |
|---|---|---|
| Organic Lettuce (MCG-GRO-LET-001) | 7.2% | Wilting on arrival -- cold chain break suspected at farm |
| Salad Mix (MCG-GRO-MIX-015) | 5.1% | Short shelf life (arriving with < 3 days to expiry) |
| Fresh Herbs | 1.8% | Acceptable |
| Microgreens | 2.0% | Acceptable |

**Root cause investigation**: Fresh Direct informed us that their primary lettuce farm (FARM-NL-0042) experienced irrigation system failures in August, leading to inconsistent crop quality. They shifted 30% of lettuce volume to a backup farm (identity not disclosed to MCG -- sub-tier visibility gap).

**NOTE**: FARM-NL-0042 is the same farm implicated in recall RCL-2025-003 (contaminated lettuce, September 2025). The irrigation system failure may have contributed to contamination conditions.

### 3.2 Lot Number and Origin Documentation

While lot number compliance improved from 88% to 91%, it remains below the 100% target mandated by MCG-POL-007. The 9% gap primarily affects products sourced from the undisclosed backup farm, which does not provide lot-level tracking to Fresh Direct.

Country of origin documentation improved from 79% to 82% but remains RED. Products from the backup farm arrive without farm-level origin ID. Fresh Direct states they are "working with the farm" to improve documentation but cannot provide a timeline.

### 3.3 Delivery Performance

On-time delivery dropped from 92% to 87%. Primary drivers:
- Increased quality inspection holds at MCG DCs (due to higher rejection rates)
- Fresh Direct's logistics provider changed routing, adding 4-6 hours to transit from Barendrecht to MCG's Munich and Berlin DCs
- 3 instances of complete delivery failure (truck no-shows) with no advance notification

## 5. Contract Renewal Recommendation

**CONDITIONAL RENEWAL** with enhanced monitoring. Fresh Direct has been a reliable supplier historically, but Q3 performance and the connection to the lettuce recall raise serious concerns. Contract renewal (due December 2025) should be contingent on:

1. Full sub-tier transparency (all farm identities disclosed)
2. Lot number compliance at 100% for 3 consecutive months
3. Quality rejection rate back below 3%
4. Participation in MCG's Supplier Transparency Program

If corrective actions are not met by December 2025, recommend initiating RFP for alternative fresh produce supplier(s) for the EU region.
