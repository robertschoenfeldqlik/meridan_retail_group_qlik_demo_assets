## 1. Purpose

Defines monitoring requirements and response procedures for temperature exceedances during cold-chain transport and storage. Non-compliance can result in product spoilage (current annual loss: $48M), regulatory violations, and customer safety risks.

## 2. Temperature Requirements

| Product Category | Storage Requirement | Min °C | Max °C | Sensor Type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fresh produce (lettuce, herbs, berries) | Chilled | 2.0 | 6.0 | Tive tracker |
| Dairy (milk, yogurt, cheese) | Chilled | 2.0 | 6.0 | Tive tracker |
| Fresh meat and fish | Chilled | 0.0 | 4.0 | Tive tracker |
| Frozen goods | Frozen | -24.0 | -18.0 | Sensitech monitor |
| Pharmaceuticals / health supplements | Controlled room temp | 15.0 | 25.0 | Warehouse sensor |

### 3.2 Warehouse Monitoring

All temperature-controlled zones are monitored by fixed sensors with 1-minute reading intervals. Alerts are generated automatically when zone temperature deviates from range for > 10 consecutive minutes.

## 4. Exceedance Response Procedure

### 4.1 In-Transit Exceedance

**Definition**: Any reading outside the product's temperature range for > 15 consecutive minutes.

1. **Automated alert** generated by IoT platform and sent to:
   - Cold Chain Manager (email + SMS)
   - Origin DC Operations Manager
   - Destination DC Operations Manager

2. **Immediate assessment** (within 30 minutes):
   - Review sensor data trend: Is temperature rising, stabilizing, or returning to range?
   - Contact carrier for explanation (equipment failure? door opened? ambient temperature?)
   - Assess duration and severity of exceedance

3. **Decision matrix**:

| Duration | Severity (°C above max) | Product | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| < 30 min | < 2°C | Non-meat | **MONITOR** -- likely to recover |
| < 30 min | < 2°C | Meat/fish | **EXPEDITE** delivery + inspect on arrival |
| 30-60 min | 2-5°C | Any | **DIVERT** to nearest DC for inspection |
| > 60 min | > 5°C | Any | **REJECT** shipment. Do not deliver. |
| > 60 min | Any | Frozen (thaw detected) | **REJECT** shipment. Product cannot be refrozen. |

4. **Documentation**: Record all exceedance events, decisions, and outcomes in the Cold Chain Incident Log.

### 4.2 Warehouse Zone Exceedance

1. Fixed sensors trigger automatic alerts at > 10 minutes out of range
2. Warehouse engineer dispatched to investigate (compressor failure, door left open, power issue)
3. If resolution ETA > 30 minutes: begin relocating affected inventory to backup zone
4. Products exposed to out-of-range temperatures for > 60 minutes must be quarantined and inspected

## 5. Spoilage Risk Scoring

The Supply Chain Analytics team is developing a predictive spoilage risk model (Project Qlarity, Qlik AutoML) that will score each shipment based on:
- Cold chain temperature history
- Time in transit
- Product shelf life remaining
- Demand velocity at destination
- Historical spoilage rates for the route

Target deployment: Q1 2026. Currently in training/validation phase.

**Current model accuracy (validation set): 82% precision at 79% recall.**

The model will surface risk scores directly in the Qlik Analytics Supply Chain Command Center dashboard and trigger proactive alerts via the Discovery Agent when a shipment's risk score exceeds the threshold.

## 6. Sao Paulo-Buenos Aires Corridor Issue (Active)

**Status: UNDER INVESTIGATION**

Since September 2025, temperature exceedance events on the GRU-BA route have increased 340%. Preliminary findings:
- Carrier (TransFrio Logistica) upgraded their fleet in August 2025
- New trucks have different refrigeration units with a 15-minute startup delay
- Long border crossing wait times at Uruguaiana (average 3.5 hours, up from 1.2 hours) expose refrigerated trailers to ambient temperatures of 32-38°C
- Estimated exposure: $180K per week in spoilage risk

**Recommended actions (pending VP Supply Chain approval):**
1. Switch high-value perishable shipments to air freight for the GRU-BA corridor (cost increase ~$45K/month)
2. Require TransFrio to pre-cool new trucks to 0°C before loading (instead of the standard 4°C)
3. Negotiate with customs for priority processing of refrigerated cargo
4. Add redundant Tive sensors to all GRU-BA shipments for dual monitoring
