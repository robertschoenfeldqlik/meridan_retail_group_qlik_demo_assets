## 1. Summary of Changes

This amendment modifies the service tier classification for parcels exceeding 5kg, effective October 1, 2025.

## 2. Service Tier Reclassification

### Previous Terms (Pre-October 1, 2025)

| Parcel Weight | Service Tier | SLA (Business Days) | Rate per Parcel (SGD) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 - 3 kg | Standard | 2 | $4.20 |
| 3 - 5 kg | Standard | 2 | $5.80 |
| 5 - 10 kg | Standard | 2 | $7.50 |
| 10 - 20 kg | Standard | 3 | $12.00 |
| > 20 kg | Heavy Parcel | 5 | Quote-based |

### New Terms (Effective October 1, 2025)

| Parcel Weight | Service Tier | SLA (Business Days) | Rate per Parcel (SGD) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 - 3 kg | Standard | 2 | $4.20 (unchanged) |
| 3 - 5 kg | Standard | 2 | $5.80 (unchanged) |
| **5 - 10 kg** | **Standard Plus** | **3** | **$8.90** |
| **10 - 20 kg** | **Heavy Parcel** | **5** | **$14.50** |
| > 20 kg | Heavy Parcel | 5 | Quote-based (unchanged) |

### Impact Summary

- Parcels between **5-10 kg** are reclassified from "Standard" (2-day SLA) to "Standard Plus" (3-day SLA)
- Rate increase of **$1.40 per parcel** for the 5-10 kg bracket
- Parcels between **10-20 kg** SLA unchanged at 5 days, but rate increases by **$2.50 per parcel**

## 3. Impact on MCG Operations

Based on Q2 2025 shipping data for Singapore:

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total monthly parcels via NinjaVan (Singapore) | 42,800 |
| Parcels in 5-10 kg bracket | 14,200 (33.2%) |
| Parcels in 10-20 kg bracket | 3,400 (7.9%) |
| **Primary affected category** | **Electronics > 5kg (speakers, small appliances, monitors)** |
| Monthly cost increase | ~$28,380 SGD |
| SLA impact | 14,200 parcels/month now have 3-day instead of 2-day SLA |

**Note from Li Wei Chen (APAC Logistics):**
> "This amendment is the primary driver of the SLA performance decline we've observed in Singapore since October. 68% of the affected orders are electronics over 5kg, which were previously delivered in 2 days and now take 3. Our checkout page still promises 2-day delivery for Singapore, which means we're systematically over-promising for approximately 14,200 orders per month. This needs urgent attention from the e-commerce team to update delivery estimates, and from Procurement to either renegotiate or source a secondary carrier for the 5-10 kg bracket."

## 4. Recommended Actions

1. **Immediate**: Update meridiancommerce.com delivery estimates for Singapore electronics > 5kg from "2 business days" to "3 business days"
2. **Short-term**: Evaluate secondary carrier options for Singapore heavy parcels (Lalamove, J&T Express)
3. **Medium-term**: Renegotiate weight brackets in Q1 2026 contract renewal, leveraging volume commitment
4. **Data**: Update carrier SLA reference table in TMS to reflect new 3-day SLA for 5-10 kg bracket
