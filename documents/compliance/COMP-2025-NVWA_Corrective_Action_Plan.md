## 1. Inspection Summary

On June 10, 2025, NVWA conducted an inspection at MCG's Rotterdam Distribution Center (DC-RTD-01) triggered by the lettuce recall (RCL-2025-003). The inspection focused on MCG's ability to comply with Article 18 of Regulation (EC) No 178/2002, which requires food businesses to identify the origin and destination of food products.

### 1.1 Findings

| Finding # | Description | Severity | Regulation Reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| F-001 | MCG was unable to provide complete lot-to-store traceability within 4 hours as required. The lettuce recall (RCL-2025-003) required **11 days** to complete full traceability. | **Major** | Art. 18(2), Reg. (EC) 178/2002 |
| F-002 | Lot number data capture at goods receipt was incomplete (85% compliance). 15% of food product receipts did not have a lot number recorded in a structured, searchable field. | **Major** | Art. 18(3), Reg. (EC) 178/2002 |
| F-003 | Country/farm of origin documentation was incomplete for 12% of fresh produce receipts at the Rotterdam DC. | **Significant** | Art. 18(2), Reg. (EC) 178/2002 |
| F-004 | Store-level receipt tracking for food products was at 70% -- 30% of product deliveries from DC to stores could not be traced to the receiving store within 4 hours. | **Significant** | Art. 18(2), Reg. (EC) 178/2002 |
| F-005 | Traceability data was fragmented across 4 separate systems (SAP, Oracle WMS, supplier portal, QMS) with no unified query capability. Manual cross-referencing required 65 person-hours for the lettuce recall. | **Observation** | Best practice |

### 1.2 Outcome

NVWA issued a **formal citation** (NVWA-2025-CIT-04412) for findings F-001 and F-002 (Major severity). A corrective action plan was required within 30 days. A follow-up inspection is planned for **Q1 2026**.

## 2. Corrective Action Plan

### Action 1: Mandatory Lot Number Capture (Immediate)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Finding Addressed** | F-002 |
| **Action** | Implement mandatory lot number capture at all MCG distribution centers for all food products. Lot number must be entered in a structured, validated field in WMS (not free-text comments). |
| **Implementation** | Updated SOP-WH-001 (Goods Receiving Procedure) v3.0 to require structured lot number capture. WMS modified to make lot number a required field for food product category receipts. |
| **Due Date** | August 10, 2025 |
| **Status** | **COMPLETED** (October 1, 2025) |
| **Evidence** | SOP-WH-001 v3.0 published. WMS validation rule active at all DCs since October 1. Compliance rate improved from 85% to 93% in first week. Target: 100% by November 2025. |

### Action 2: Store-Level Receipt Tracking Improvement (60 Days)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Finding Addressed** | F-004 |
| **Action** | Improve store-level receipt tracking for food products to > 85%. Implement barcode scanning at store receiving for all food deliveries. |
| **Implementation** | Deployed handheld scanners to all EU stores (Phase 1: NL, DE, BE). Updated store receiving SOP to require scan of delivery manifest barcode at receipt. |
| **Due Date** | September 10, 2025 |
| **Status** | **IN PROGRESS** -- Currently at 78% (up from 70%) |
| **Evidence** | Scanner deployment complete for NL and DE stores. BE and FR stores in Phase 2 (November). Weekly compliance reports generated from SAP. |
| **Revised Target** | 85% by December 2025, 95% by March 2026 |

### Action 3: Unified Traceability System Plan (90 Days)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Finding Addressed** | F-001, F-005 |
| **Action** | Provide NVWA with a documented plan for implementing a unified traceability system that can provide end-to-end product trace within 24 hours. |
| **Implementation** | Project CLARITY (Qlik platform) business case developed. Platform provides unified data integration across SAP, WMS, supplier portal, and QMS with Qlik Answers enabling natural language traceability queries. |
| **Due Date** | October 10, 2025 |
| **Status** | **IN PROGRESS** -- Business case submitted to CIO, presentation scheduled October 31 |
| **Evidence** | Project CLARITY Business Case (PROJ-2025-CLARITY). Qlik platform selected as strategic partner. Phase 1 includes CDC integration and unified traceability dashboard. |
| **Timeline** | Phase 1 go-live: Q2 2026 (unified traceability dashboard). Full Qlik Answers integration: Q4 2026. |

### Action 4: Sub-Tier Supplier Mapping (12 Months)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Finding Addressed** | F-003 (and broader supply chain visibility) |
| **Action** | Map all sub-tier suppliers (farms, processors) for high-risk food categories. Achieve 80% visibility by December 2026. |
| **Implementation** | Launched Supplier Transparency Program. Requires all Tier 1 food suppliers to disclose sub-tier suppliers by contract amendment. |
| **Due Date** | June 2026 (interim milestone: 50% by December 2025) |
| **Status** | **IN PROGRESS** -- Currently at 36% visibility (430 of ~1,200 estimated sub-tier suppliers mapped) |
| **Evidence** | Supplier Transparency Program charter. Fresh Direct NL corrective action (SUPP-2025-FreshDirect) requires full sub-tier disclosure by November 15, 2025. |

### Action 5: Country/Farm of Origin Documentation (90 Days)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Finding Addressed** | F-003 |
| **Action** | Achieve 100% country of origin documentation for all food products received at MCG DCs. |
| **Implementation** | Updated supplier requirements to mandate country and facility of origin on all shipping documents. WMS validation rule added. Supplier non-compliance triggers automatic hold at receiving. |
| **Due Date** | October 10, 2025 |
| **Status** | **IN PROGRESS** -- Currently at 88% (up from 82% at inspection) |
| **Evidence** | WMS validation rule active. Non-compliant receipts automatically flagged. Weekly reports to Compliance team. |
| **Challenge** | Several LATAM and Africa suppliers lack farm-level traceability. Engagement ongoing through Supplier Transparency Program. |

## 4. Q1 2026 Inspection Readiness

For the anticipated NVWA follow-up inspection in Q1 2026, MCG will be prepared to demonstrate:

1. **Lot number compliance at 100%** (mandatory field in WMS, no free-text entries)
2. **Store receipt tracking at > 85%** (scanner-based verification at store level)
3. **Country of origin at > 95%** (WMS validation + supplier enforcement)
4. **Project CLARITY in active implementation** (Phase 1 infrastructure deployed, first dashboards live)
5. **Sub-tier mapping at > 50%** (with roadmap to 80% by end of 2026)

**Key risk**: If Project CLARITY CIO approval is delayed beyond November 2025, MCG will not be able to demonstrate an active unified traceability implementation at the Q1 2026 inspection. This would significantly weaken our position with NVWA.
